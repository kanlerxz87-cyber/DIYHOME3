Link register — product-intent confirmation

For each submitted link we record the intended ASIN, the official listing URL, the confirmation date, and the source. The observed redirect target is stored in a separate column and is never merged with the intended ASIN. Rationale: Amazon's ASIN definition provides a checkable identifier — the redirect observation is evidence, not identity.

Why separate fields? The submitted short-link codes (B0hmGG4m2, B03QDuGdo, B0hpD2JGQ) do not equal the destination ASINs. Merging them would misidentify the products. We do not claim the supplied short links are invalid — their exact compliance status is unresolved (see Flagged for Review).

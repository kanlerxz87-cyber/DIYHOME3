Campaign ledger

Empty by design. This ledger records only values obtained from authorized first-party reports (Associates Central reports, Search Console). No conversion score and no revenue forecast is presented here — measurement comes before automation.

How rows get added

  • Timestamp: date the report was exported (UTC).
  • Source: the report name (e.g., “Associates Central — Links Performance”, “Search Console — Performance”).
  • Channel: where the traffic came from (organic search, direct, social).
  • Eligible sessions / Clicks / Reported orders: copied verbatim from the report — never estimated, never blended.
  • Provenance: link or filename of the exported report, kept with the repo.

Our own privacy-preserving click counts (outbound_click events) are operational data, not campaign results — they live in data/events.jsonl via the self-hosted endpoint and are never presented as orders or revenue.

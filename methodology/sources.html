S02 Official

Associates Program Policies (Commission Income Statement, Participation Requirements, IP License)

Amazon.com Associates Central · Updated April 14, 2026 · checked 2026-11-20 · open source

Access: Public — retrieved successfully in this session (chunks 0, 1 and 8).

Quotation relied upon: “a link that sends users indirectly to an Amazon Site via an intermediate site, without requiring the customer to click on a link or take some other affirmative action on that intermediate site (a “Redirecting Link”)”

Used for:

  • Commission Income Statement §1(b) — a Session ends at the first of: 24 hours from the click, the customer placing a non-digital order, or clicking through someone else’s Special Link.
  • Commission Income Statement §1(c)(i) — cart-add purchases qualify if completed no later than 89 days after the initial click-through.
  • Commission Income Statement §1(c)(iii) — the product must be shipped/streamed and paid for within 180 days of purchase.
  • Commission Income Statement §2(e) — purchases from a "Redirecting Link" are disqualified.
  • Commission Income Statement §2(g) — purchases "not correctly tracked or reported because the links from your site to the relevant Amazon Site are not properly formatted" are disqualified.
  • Commission Income Statement §2(c) — Prohibited Paid Search Placement (no bidding on "amazon", "kindle" or Amazon Marks).
  • Commission Income Statement §2(d) — purchases referred by a link generated or displayed on a search engine are disqualified.
  • Commission Income Statement §3 — Standard Commission Income rates live in the Appendix of this page.
  • IP License §2(h) — ASINs may be stored indefinitely; Product Advertising Content images may not be stored or cached, and a LINK to such an image may be stored for only up to 24 hours.
  • IP License §2(i) — pricing/availability displays need a date/time stamp plus a specific disclaimer sentence.
  • IP License §2(k) — a specific "comes from Amazon ... subject to change or removal" disclaimer is required when displaying Program Content consisting of text.
  • IP License §4 — Agent Terms: an autonomous agent must identify itself with "Agent/[agent name]" in the user-agent string and must not conceal that its access is automated.

S10 Official

Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, 16 CFR Part 255 (2023 revision)

U.S. Federal Trade Commission · Final rule; adoption of revised Guides (2023) · checked 2026-11-20 · open source

Access: Public — text retrieved via search index extract in this session; PDF not downloaded.

Quotation relied upon: “Endorsements must reflect the honest opinions, findings, beliefs, or experience of the endorser. Furthermore, an endorsement may not convey any express or implied representation that would be deceptive if made directly by the advertiser.”

Used for:

  • §255.1(a) — no endorsement may assert experience the endorser does not have. This is the authority for refusing to publish first-person "I tested this" copy on an automated build.
  • §255.3 — expert endorsements require that the endorser actually possess the claimed expertise. This is the authority for leaving authorship as a named-desk placeholder pending a real human reviewer.
  • §255.5 — material connections must be clearly and conspicuously disclosed.

Compliance

Compliance hub

Four obligations apply to this site, from four different directions. They are separated here because they have different owners and different failure modes, and because conflating them is how affiliate sites end up compliant with one and in breach of another.

1 · Disclosure The Amazon Associates statement, its exact wording, and where it appears on every page. Plus the FTC clear-and-conspicuous standard. 2 · Associates programme rules Special Link format, disqualifying purchase types, attribution windows, IP License limits on images, prices and caching, and the Agent Terms. 3 · FTC endorsement guidance 16 CFR Part 255: honest experience, expert qualification, material connections. Why this site makes no experience claim at all. 4 · Privacy and data No cookies, no third-party scripts, no personal data. What the first-party counter does and does not record.

The gate

Compliance is in the build, not beside it

Thirteen checks run in CI on every push and pull request and fail the build on a violation. Seven further obligations cannot be checked by a machine at all, because they need an authenticated Associates Central session or a legal judgement. Both lists are published in full, with the current state of each, on the compliance gate dashboard.

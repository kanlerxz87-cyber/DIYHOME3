Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

What that means in plain terms

This site participates in the Amazon Associates programme, an affiliate advertising programme. When a reader follows a product link from here to Amazon and then buys something, the operator of this site may receive a commission. The price the reader pays is not affected by arriving via this site.

Every outbound product-intent link on this site is also marked in the HTML with rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer", which tells search engines that the link exists because of a commercial arrangement rather than because it is an independent editorial recommendation. S12

The current state of attribution — read this

No link on this site currently carries a tracking tag

As published, every product link points at a canonical, untagged Amazon listing. The disclosure above is displayed because the intent of these links is commercial and because the site is built to participate in the programme. But no click currently attributes to shopnow047c-20, because the supplied links resolved without any tag parameter and no tagged Special Link has yet been validated in Associates Central. S09

This is stated openly rather than left implicit. A reader who clicks a product link here is not currently generating a commission for this site. F-01 · Link management.

The exact wording, and where it comes from

You must clearly and prominently state the following, or any substantially similar statement previously allowed under this Agreement, on your Site or any other location where Amazon may authorize your display or other use of Program Content: “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” Associates Program Operating Agreement, Section 5 — Identifying Yourself as an Associate. Updated October 15, 2025. S01

Section 6 of the same Agreement adds the consequence: “any violation of Section 5 and as specified in the Program Policies will be deemed a material breach of this Agreement.” S01

Placement is enforced structurally: the disclosure strip is rendered by the page shell itself, so no individual page can omit it. tools/verify.mjs additionally asserts that the sentence appears before <main> in every built HTML file.

Two further required disclaimers

Both are reproduced verbatim in the footer of every page, because Amazon’s IP License requires them where Program Content or pricing is displayed. S02

IP License §2(k) — required where Program Content consisting of text is displayed:

CERTAIN CONTENT THAT APPEARS ON THIS SITE COMES FROM AMAZON. THIS CONTENT IS PROVIDED ‘AS IS’ AND IS SUBJECT TO CHANGE OR REMOVAL AT ANY TIME.

IP License §2(i) — required adjacent to any pricing or availability display:

Product prices and availability are accurate as of the date/time indicated and are subject to change. Any price and availability information displayed on amazon.com at the time of purchase will apply to the purchase of this product.

Every price on this site also carries a date/time stamp in the form Amazon’s own guidance illustrates: “Amazon.com Price: $5.08 (as of 2026-11-20 06:00 UTC — Details)”.

What this site never says

  • That Amazon sponsors, endorses or supports this site. Operating Agreement §5 forbids misrepresenting or embellishing the relationship.
  • That any product was tested, owned or used by anyone here. Nobody tested anything for this site.
  • That using these links supports a charity, a cause or a community. Amazon’s guidance prohibits offering any consideration or incentive for using the links.
  • Anything about the Operating Agreement beyond the disclosure itself, without advance written permission — §5 restricts public communication about the Agreement. Quoting it here for operational purposes may itself need permission; that question is left with the owner rather than resolved unilaterally.

About & methodology

DIY Home Upgrades is a small publication covering DIY home products. We lead with the products, not with the verification process — but the verification is what makes the products trustworthy.

Authorship

Written and maintained by the DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk (site owner). Product specs are transcribed from official Amazon listing pages on the verification date shown. We do not claim hands-on testing we haven't done: where a product hasn't been tested by us, the guide says so.

How we verify

  • Every spec traces to https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN> on the date shown.
  • Intended ASIN and observed redirect target are separate fields — never merged.
  • Values we can't confirm stay blank and move to Flagged for Review.
  • Prices and ratings are snapshots; the live listing always wins.

How guides are written

Guides are question-shaped: they start from the buyer's question, give a checklist usable at the listing page, and link the verified pick. Working titles are hypotheses — not claims about popularity or superiority. New topics are chosen from measured search queries once analytics data exists, not from instinct.

Search Console

To verify this site in Google Search Console, add your verification token as a <meta name="google-site-verification"> tag in the <head> of index.html, or submit sitemap.xml in Search Console. The sitemap is the single source of truth for indexing.

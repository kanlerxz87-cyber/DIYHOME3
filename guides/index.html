Publishing cadence

ElementSetting
StatementOne new or materially revised guide per week, published on Thursdays, with every publication adding an entry to sitemap.xml and feed.xml in the same commit.
Why it holds without disciplineThe brief asks for a cadence and for each new guide to reach the sitemap and feed. Because the site generator derives both files from site/data/guides.mjs, "in the same commit" is a property of the build rather than a discipline someone has to remember.
What publication is measured byOutbound clicks and reader-capture events from the site’s own analytics endpoint, and — once Search Console access is granted — by the queries that actually bring arrivals. No topic in this build was chosen from search-volume data, because no authorised search-volume source was available.
How the next topic is chosenThe next guide is chosen from measured queries, not from instinct. Until measurement is live, the queue is held at the four hypothesis titles already supplied in the brief and no new topics are invented.

Every guide appears in sitemap.xml and feed.xml automatically — both are generated from the same registry as these pages, so there is no second list to forget. Subscribe: https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/DIYHOME3/feed.xml

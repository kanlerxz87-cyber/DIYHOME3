What should you ask before buying a multi-function appliance?

Start with the functions you’ll actually use and check what each one needs before giving up counter space.

By the DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · November 20, 2026 · Desk research, not hands-on testing

Name the jobs you want it to replace

Write down the meals and tasks that matter to you. Then compare the manual’s mode instructions, available accessories and useful dimensions. A preset on a control panel isn’t a replacement for the space your current appliance offers.

Instant Pot · Vortex 6QT specifications

Check what stays on the counter

Ask which accessories you will have to store separately and how you’ll wash them. Compare the whole setup with the tools you already own, not just the footprint on the box.

Check meal instructions before relying on presets

Follow package instructions where available and use a thermometer for safe internal temperatures. USDA specifically says not to air fry raw stuffed breaded chicken breast products.

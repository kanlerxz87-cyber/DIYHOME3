What should you compare before buying peel-and-stick wallpaper tools?

A smoothing kit looks simple — a squeegee and a grip. The differences that matter are the edge, the handle, and what the box doesn't include. Use this checklist at the listing page.

The no-hype checklist

  • Edge shape. A straight, flexible edge pushes air out evenly; a curved edge can leave streaks on flat walls. Match the edge to your material — wallpaper vs. vinyl tile.
  • Grip comfort. You'll hold it for long runs. An ergonomic grip matters more than the brand name. Check the listing for grip description and weight (ours: 4.2 ounces).
  • What's in the box. Basic kits include the squeegee and handle. They usually do not include a utility knife, seam roller, or trimming guide — budget for those separately.
  • Material. Plastic tools won't scratch most peel-and-stick surfaces. Metal edges can — avoid them on printed vinyl.
  • Rating context. A 4.6★ average across 26,000+ ratings (our pick) tells you more than a 5.0★ across 40 ratings. Always check the count, not just the stars.

Adhesive or drilling: what should you check before mounting anything in a kitchen?

Kitchens are the worst environment in the house for an adhesive mount. Here is the checklist that decides.

By DIYHOME3 Editorial Desk Automated research and drafting; pending named human review Published 2026-11-20 Season 2026 holiday returns window Editorial standards

Why kitchens are hard on adhesive

Three things cycle in a kitchen that do not cycle elsewhere: temperature from cooking, steam and humidity from boiling and dishwashing, and grease film from frying. All three work against an adhesive bond. Grease film is the quietest of them — a surface that looks clean can still carry enough of it to prevent a proper bond.

The pre-mount checklist

  • Degrease the mounting surface with a solvent cleaner and let it fully dry. Wiping with a damp cloth is not degreasing.
  • Identify the substrate: solid timber, plywood, particle board, painted plaster, tile, glass, or a hollow panel.
  • Confirm the surface is not textured. Adhesive pads bond to area, and texture removes area.
  • Check the temperature. Most adhesive mounts want a warm, dry surface at application time, not a cold one.
  • Observe the cure time before loading. Adhesive mounts generally need to sit unloaded before they carry anything.
  • Decide the load case: static weight, or repeated pulling force.

The decision, stated simply

Your situationChooseBecause
Rental, light static load, smooth non-porous surfaceAdhesiveReversible, no holes, and the load case is forgiving
Repeated pulling force (tearing a roll, loading wet pans)ScrewsAdhesive creeps under cyclic shear load
Textured, porous or greasy-prone surfaceScrewsAdhesive cannot bond to enough area
Above a hob or a boiling zoneScrewsHeat and steam cycle daily against the bond
You may remove it within a yearAdhesive, if the load case allowsScrew mounts leave anchor holes to patch and repaint

What to look for in a listing

Listings that offer both modes are common. The question is whether the stated capacity applies to both, or only to the screw mode. If the listing does not separate them, treat the adhesive capacity as unverified — and plan for screws if the load is anything other than static and light.

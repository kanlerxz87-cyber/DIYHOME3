Under-cabinet or wall-mounted: which paper towel holder fits your kitchen?

The best holder is the one you can mount without regret. These are the questions to ask before you choose adhesive or drilling — and before you measure.

The mounting checklist

  • Surface first. Adhesive needs a smooth, clean, non-porous surface (painted wood, metal, glass). Textured or dusty surfaces won't hold. Drilling needs a stud or solid backing.
  • Clearance. Under a cabinet, the roll hangs below the cabinet face. Measure the gap between cabinet bottom and counter — the roll's diameter plus spin room must fit.
  • Rail length vs. roll size. Our pick has a 12.2″ rail. Measure your largest roll's width — oversized rolls need a longer rail.
  • Material. Aluminum is lighter than stainless steel and won't rust. Matte black coating hides fingerprints but shows dust — decide which you prefer.
  • One-hand use. Under-cabinet holders let you tear with one hand while holding a pan. Wall-mounted holders need reach. Think about where you actually use towels.

Will an 8-tier pot organizer actually fit your cabinet?

An organizer that doesn't fit is just clutter with a receipt. Take these five measurements first — they take two minutes and save a return.

The five measurements

  • 1. Cabinet opening height. Our pick is 16.61″ tall. Measure from shelf to the underside of the shelf above — including the door swing.
  • 2. Cabinet depth. The rack is 11.75″ long. Measure front-to-back inside the cabinet.
  • 3. Your largest pan. The rack fits lightweight cookware up to 11″. Measure your biggest pan including handles — if it's over 11″, this rack isn't for it.
  • 4. Tier count vs. cookware count. 8 tiers sounds like a lot. Count your pots, pans, lids, and boards — if you have more than 8 items, you'll need a second rack or a different mode.
  • 5. Mode. The rack has 3 DIY modes (cabinet, shelf, countertop). Decide where it lives before you buy — the mode determines the footprint.

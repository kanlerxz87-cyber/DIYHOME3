Kitchen organization

Adhesive or screws for an under-cabinet paper towel holder?

Before you pick a mount, check the cabinet underside, the roll you buy and how much room you need to swap it.

By the DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · November 20, 2026 · Desk research, not hands-on testing

Illustration of the category, not the observed listing.

Choose the spot before the mount

Hold a full roll where it would sit. Check that the cabinet door still swings and that you can slide off the empty tube without moving appliances. A low-profile holder still needs room at the open end.

Amazon · Observed paper towel holder listing

Read the instructions for both mounting options

The observed listing names adhesive and drilling as options. Check the instructions for compatible surfaces and curing time before using adhesive. If using screws, check panel thickness and confirm you can safely fix into the material.

Amazon · Observed paper towel holder listing

Think about removing it later

If the space is rented, check what alterations are permitted. Don’t assume adhesive will come off cleanly or that screws supplied for one surface will suit another.

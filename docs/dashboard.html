The machinery, and its gate

  1. Collect — submitted links land in data/products.csv verbatim, with provenance. Duplicates are counted, not silently dropped.
  2. Validate (automated) — scripts/validate_register.py checks token/ASIN shape (10 characters, source S-05), duplicate tokens, and that intended and observed fields are never merged. It cannot confirm intent.
  3. Confirm (human gate) — the owner confirms intended ASIN + official listing, then generates one Special Link per product in the enrolled Associates workflow (S-01/S-02). Automation stops here on purpose.
  4. Test — that one link's destination and the Associates tracking report are checked before any scaling. Only then does a shop card get a tracked CTA, still marked Sponsored with rel="sponsored" (S-06).
  5. Publish — pages are generated from the CSVs (scripts/generate_pages.py), feeds rebuilt (scripts/build_feeds.py), and scripts/check_site.py fails the build if any page lacks the disclosure strip, any outbound product link lacks rel="sponsored", or any internal link is broken.
  6. Measure — five privacy-preserving events (see Analytics) and an empty-until-sourced campaign ledger. No “AI trending score”, commission forecast or revenue claim exists anywhere in this system, because no official data source for one is connected (F-08).

What “AI-powered research” honestly means here

Research assistance (collecting submitted links, drafting question checklists, generating pages and checks) is automated. Product selection, pricing, review, competition and commission claims are not automated and are not asserted: they require the owner's Associates/PA-API access and reports. Anything else would be invention, so the gate above is the product.

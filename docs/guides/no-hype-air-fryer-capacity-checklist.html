A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

By the DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · Published 2026-11-20 · Guide (no products are ranked in this article)

A working-title editorial hypothesis from the project brief. Capacity numbers are not compared here — this is the checklist to apply on official listings.

Questions to work through

  1. Is capacity quoted in litres/quarts, and is it basket volume or usable cooking volume? Note the definition each brand uses.
  2. What is the largest single item you cook (whole chicken, pizza, tray)? Compare its dimensions to the stated interior dimensions.
  3. Does the stated capacity assume one layer? If you regularly cook for more people, how would a second batch change your cooking time?
  4. Are accessories (racks, trays) included or sold separately, per the official 'what's in the box' list?
How to use this guide: answer each question from the official Amazon listing or the manufacturer's own documentation, and write the answer down with its source. If a listing does not state a figure, leave it blank and treat it as unverified — exactly as we do in the product register.

Field definitions

  • timestamp — when the reported period ends / the report was obtained (ISO 8601).
  • source — the exact report the numbers came from (e.g. “Associates Central earnings report export”).
  • channel — where the clicks originated (this site, a named guide, RSS).
  • eligible sessions / clicks / reported orders — copied from that report only; never modelled or interpolated.
  • provenance note — who exported it, when, and where the export is filed.

About

Authorship, brand and editorial standards

Who publishes this

DIYHOME3 is an Amazon Associates property covering reversible, low-cost home upgrades. It is published as a static site from a public repository, with every claim traceable to a source and every gap marked rather than filled.

The byline is a desk, not a person — on purpose

Every article on this site is bylined DIYHOME3 Editorial Desk and labelled Automated research and drafting; pending named human review. No named human has tested, owned or used any product in this register for this site.

FTC 16 CFR §255.1(a) requires that an endorsement reflect the honest opinions, findings, beliefs or experience of the endorser, and §255.3 requires that where an advertisement presents the endorser as an expert, the endorser must actually have that expertise. Amazon’s Operating Agreement §5 separately forbids misrepresenting or embellishing the relationship with Amazon. Inventing a persona with a biography would violate all three at once, so no persona exists. S10 S01

This byline is a desk, not a person, on purpose. No article in this build carries a first-person experience claim, because FTC 16 CFR §255.1(a) ties endorsements to the endorser’s actual experience and no human has tested these products for this site. Assigning a named reviewer is the first open action on the Flagged for Review page. Flag F-14.

Brand

Name and mark

DIYHOME3. The mark is an inline SVG — a roofline over a doorway with a fixing point at the centre. It is drawn in code, so it costs no HTTP request and no licence.

Wordmark set in a system serif; body in a system sans. No webfont is loaded, because a font request is a third-party dependency and a privacy question.

Imagery policy

No Amazon product photography appears anywhere on this site, and no synthetic product imagery is generated either — an invented picture of a real ASIN would misrepresent it. All imagery is original abstract artwork describing the category, never a product.

Licensing basis: IP License §2(h). S02 See F-09.

What this site is not

  • Not a storefront. It leads with questions and evidence, not with buy buttons. Every product page states plainly that attribution is inactive.
  • Not a review site. It publishes comparison criteria. No verdict, no star rating of its own, no ranking.
  • Not an endorsement. No human here has used these products, and the copy never implies otherwise.
  • Not a forecast. No revenue projection, no conversion score, no traffic estimate, anywhere.
  • Not affiliated with Amazon beyond the Associates relationship. Nothing on this site implies sponsorship, endorsement or support by Amazon.

Contact and corrections

Corrections are handled in the open. Every page is generated from a public repository, so a factual error can be raised as an issue and fixed in the data file that generated it — one edit, and the correction reaches the page, the register, the sitemap and the feed together.

No contact email address is published, because none was supplied and inventing one would create an inbox nobody reads. That is recorded as an open item rather than papered over with a plausible address.

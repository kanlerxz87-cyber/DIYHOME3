Why these two fields are kept apart

The brief requires that for each submitted link the record stores the intended ASIN, the official listing URL, a confirmation date and a source, and that observed redirect targets are kept in a separate field. Merging them would destroy the only evidence that the link and the product disagree. On this register they disagree on all three products: the submitted tokens are not ASINs at all, and the ASINs observed at the destination were never asserted by anyone.

What this does and does not prove

It proves that on one date, from one session, in one marketplace, these three short links resolved to these three listings carrying no tracking tag, by way of a search-fallback path with a per-request token.

It does not prove that link.amazon is non-compliant, that the mapping is unstable, or that the links are broken. The brief is explicit that the supplied short links must not be called invalid and that their compliance status is unresolved. This record states the observation and stops there.

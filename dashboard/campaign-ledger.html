Campaign ledger

Dashboard

This ledger is empty on purpose

The brief asks for an empty but well-defined campaign ledger, with timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks and reported orders added only when obtained from authorised first-party reports, and a dated provenance trail. It asks for no conversion score and no revenue forecast. A ledger pre-filled with plausible zeroes would be indistinguishable from a real one after a few weeks, so nothing is pre-filled.

⚠ Human gate — not automatable

Only the account holder can export an authorised Associates report, and only they can say which report a figure came from. Provenance is not recoverable after the fact.

Required action: When you export a report, record its exact name and export date alongside the figures. Enter rows only from that export. Never reconcile a row against a remembered number.

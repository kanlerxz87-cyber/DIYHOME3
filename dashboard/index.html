All surfaces

Overview One-screen status of the DIYHOME3 affiliate operation: what is verified, what is gated, and what is waiting on a human. Product research Every supplied link, its resolution, and the verified attributes of the listing it landed on, each with an official source. Link intent Intended identifier and observed redirect target held in separate fields, never merged, each with its own source and capture date. Pricing Observed prices with their anchor labels, discount basis, membership gating and capture timestamp, per Amazon IP License §2(i). Reviews Amazon-computed rating aggregates as observed, with the boundary of what this site will and will not do with them. Competition What can honestly be said about competitive position from the data available, and what cannot be said at all. Commission The commission rate is blank for every product, with the conflicting third-party figures shown as evidence for why, and a calculator that takes the rate as an input. Niche The niche this product set actually occupies, derived from observed Amazon browse-node paths rather than from an assumption. Content pipeline The published guide set, the cadence, the cluster mapping, and the rules that keep generated copy from asserting things nobody verified. Link management Current link state per product, the candidate Special Link format, and the six-step validation that must pass before any link is monetised. Click tracking The privacy-preserving outbound-click counter, the self-hosted collector, the five recorded events and exactly what is and is not collected. Conversion The attribution model Amazon actually applies, what data would be needed to measure it, and why no conversion figure is published. Campaign ledger An empty but fully specified ledger: fixed schema, provenance requirements, validation rules, and no invented rows. Compliance gate The disclosure and policy checks that run in the build, the ones that need a human, and the current pass state of each. SEO & findability One indexable domain, a generated sitemap and feed, canonical URLs, robots.txt, and the honest state of Search Console. Automation What is automated end to end, what is automated with a human checkpoint, and what is gated behind a human by design.

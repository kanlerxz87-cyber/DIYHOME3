What this site deliberately does not do

  • No review text is quoted. Not one customer review is reproduced anywhere on this site. Individual reviews are shopper-authored content, quoting them would create endorsement obligations under FTC 16 CFR §255, and none were collected in this session.
  • No star histogram is published. The five-bar breakdown was not captured, and inventing one from an average would be fabrication.
  • No "verified purchase" proportion is claimed. Not observed, therefore not published.
  • No banded figure is converted to a precise one. "20K+ bought in past month" is reproduced as the banded string Amazon rendered. It is never written as 20,000.
  • No badge is described as an award, a ranking or a test result. It is a merchandising signal with Amazon’s own stated definition, quoted above.
  • No rating is treated as a quality measurement. It is an aggregate of shopper opinion.
Ratings drift, and this page will not notice

These are single-observation snapshots from one date. A static page cannot detect that a rating has moved. Any reuse of these figures in promotional copy should be preceded by a fresh observation with a new capture date, not by trusting the number here.

Why no revenue forecast appears anywhere on this site

Amazon’s own Operating Agreement states that it makes "no representation, warranty, or covenant regarding the amount of traffic or commission income you can expect at any time in connection with the Associates Program". The brief separately requires that no conversion score or revenue forecast be presented. Both point the same way, so this site publishes observed inputs and arithmetic, and nothing that resembles a projection.

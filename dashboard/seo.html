Emitting rich-result markup you cannot substantiate is a common way to lose a rich result entirely. Withholding it is the safer and more honest choice.

The reason to rank, stated honestly

  • Eleven guides written as questions a buyer would actually ask, each one a genuine framework rather than a product pitch.
  • A published verification method, with every factual claim carrying a link to an official source. That is rare in this niche and it is a real differentiator.
  • Blank cells where data could not be confirmed, instead of confident numbers. A buyer who has been burned by invented specifications will value this.
  • Two live topic clusters built from the observed browse-node structure, with pillars and supporting guides linked both ways.
  • A seasonal calendar anchored to a verified date — Amazon’s own 2026 holiday returns window — rather than to a guessed demand curve.
What is NOT a reason to rank, and is not claimed

No keyword research was performed, because no authorised keyword source was available. No title on this site was chosen from search-volume data. No ranking, traffic or impression figure is published anywhere. Every title is an editorial hypothesis, and the guides say so where it matters.

⚠ Human gate — not automatable

Search Console verification needs an account this session does not have and should not be given. Until it is connected there is no query data, so the next topic cannot be chosen from measured queries as the brief requires.

Required action: Verify the GitHub Pages property in Search Console, submit /sitemap.xml, and wait for impressions to accumulate. Then choose the next guide from the queries that actually bring arrivals, and resist backfilling a keyword rationale onto titles written without one.

Conversion tracking

Dashboard

No conversion data exists, and none can be synthesised

Amazon reports qualifying purchases only through Associates Central to the enrolled account holder. Nothing in this session had access to that, so there is no conversion figure, no conversion rate and no conversion score anywhere on this site. What follows is the method, defined in advance so that the first authorised report can be entered against a fixed schema.

The chain breaks at link five. Everything after it depends on the tracking tag, which is why F-01 is rated critical rather than merely high.

Why a click-to-order rate would be misleading even with data

  • The 89-day cart window means a click today can produce an order next quarter, so any short-window rate is structurally understated.
  • Last-click attribution means another publisher can end your session, so your measured rate depends on competitors you cannot see.
  • Disqualifications are applied by Amazon in its sole discretion and reported after the fact, so a rate computed from clicks and reported orders mixes qualified and disqualified activity.
  • Amazon states it makes no representation about the traffic or commission income an associate can expect, so a rate observed on three products over one period is not a rate that generalises.
The rule this surface follows

Measure before optimising. The schema is fixed now, the ledger is empty now, and the first real number goes in only when an authorised report produces it. No placeholder zero is entered in the meantime, because a zero and a blank look identical once a table has been filled in for a while.

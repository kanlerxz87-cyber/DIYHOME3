The scalability argument, and its limit

The architecture does scale: adding a product is one object in the register, adding a guide is one object in the guides array, and both propagate to the HTML, the sitemap, the feed, the cluster pages, the front page and the dashboard without a second edit. The compliance gate travels with them automatically.

But scaling the machinery is not scaling the operation

Seven of the twenty capabilities above need a human, and every one of them sits on the path to earning money. A site can grow to five hundred products and still earn nothing if the tracking tag is never validated. Automating the wrong half of this problem is the most likely way for it to fail, so the split is published here rather than left implicit.

Executive summary

What was built, what was verified, and what needs a human

A complete, live, compliant Amazon Associates property built around three verified products — with its monetisation deliberately switched off until a human validates the links.

The headline

The site is finished and the earning is not

Four links were supplied. Three were unique — two were byte-identical. All three resolved to real Amazon listings, and every listing attribute was read off the page and recorded with its source. But not one of the resolved URLs carried a tracking tag, and shopnow047c-20 does not appear anywhere in the observed redirect chain. As published, every click this site sends to Amazon earns nothing.

That is a one-line fix per product once a human has generated and validated a tagged Special Link in Associates Central. It cannot be automated, because it needs an authenticated session and a tracking report to confirm against. Rather than fake it, the site ships untagged with the gate visible on every product page.

The niche the data actually supports

Reversible, low-cost home upgrades for people who cannot or will not make permanent changes.

Read off the evidence rather than chosen in advance. All three listing titles or subheads carry a no-permanent-change signal — peel-and-stick, self-adhesive, tool-free, no-drill, under-cabinet. The set straddles two Amazon top-level departments, which a single-room framing would get wrong, but two of three sit in one narrow leaf: Storage & Organization › Racks & Holders. The price band is $5.08 to $17.74. The unifying buyer constraint is reversibility, not room and not price. S06 S07 S08

The seven decisions that need a human

  1. Confirm the three observed ASINs are the products you meant. Nothing supplied asserted an identity, and all three resolved through a search fallback seeded with "diy home upgrades". If any is wrong, that is the single most important finding in this build. F-02, F-03
  2. Validate one tagged Special Link per product in Associates Central and confirm it in the tracking report. Then flip one field per product and rebuild. F-01, F-15
  3. Confirm shopnow047c-20 is active and that the live GitHub Pages URL is registered on the account. F-16
  4. Read the commission rate off the official Appendix under your own session and enter it. The calculator has no default and will not guess one. F-05
  5. Assign a named human editor, have them read all eleven guides, and only then put their name in the byline. F-14
  6. Deploy the analytics collector to any Node host and set the endpoint. Until then every count is local to one browser and there is no aggregate measurement. F-11
  7. Decide what to do with the air-fryer cluster. The brief supplied those titles; none of the three products is a cooking appliance. They are published quarantined and labelled. F-06

One structural idea worth keeping

Every page, the sitemap, the feed, the register and the dashboard are generated from three data files. Correcting a fact is one edit and it propagates everywhere at once, including into the compliance assertions. Adding a product is one object; adding a guide is one object. That is what makes the compliance gate travel with the content instead of sitting beside it — and it is the part of this build that will still be useful when the register holds fifty products instead of three.
